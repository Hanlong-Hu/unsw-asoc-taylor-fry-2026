The levers work

Lifeguards, drones, campaigns and rip flags all go with fewer severe incidents. First aid and PA systems don’t.

Association, not proof. Patrols may go where risk is already lower, so we size their effect cautiously.

~35 s. Each dot is how much one change multiplies expected severe incidents, holding everything else fixed; the line is the 95% range. Swell is the big one: each extra metre roughly doubles severe risk; a higher tourist share and more rips push it up too. On the left are the levers Task 2 can buy: ten times the lifeguard hours cuts risk by about 30%, going from no drones to 100 hours a month by about 70%, a summer campaign by about 60%, rip flags by about 35%. First aid capacity and PA systems sit on the 1× line, so we do not fund them. Be upfront: this is association, not proof.
