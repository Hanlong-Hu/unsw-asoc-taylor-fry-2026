~35 s. Dots right of the line raise risk, dots left lower it. Swell is the big one: each extra metre roughly doubles severe risk; rips and a high tourist share push it up too. Lifeguard and drone hours, campaigns and rip flags go with lower severe risk, and they are what Task 2 buys. First aid capacity and PA systems show no effect on severe incidents, so we do not fund them. Be upfront: this is association, not proof.
