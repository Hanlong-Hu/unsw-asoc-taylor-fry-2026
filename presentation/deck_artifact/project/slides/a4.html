Q&A backup. Use if asked how the mix was chosen, what constraints were imposed, or what happens with more or less money. The optimiser picks hours and purchases to minimise expected severe incidents under the Task 1 model. Hours enter through log(1 + hours), so the first hours do the most and returns fall fast: the first half million already avoids about 8.5, and a third million adds less than one. If the budget were cut, fund drones and campaigns first.
