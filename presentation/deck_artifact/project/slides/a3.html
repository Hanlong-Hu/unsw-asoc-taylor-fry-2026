A3

Q&A backup. Use if asked where the 15–33 range comes from or why it is so wide. Each simulated year draws the model’s effects from their estimated uncertainty, one past year’s weather for the whole coast, and chance for each beach-month. Severe incidents are rare, so chance alone makes the range wide; the plan should be judged on how much it lowers the expected number, not on whether one year comes in under a target.
