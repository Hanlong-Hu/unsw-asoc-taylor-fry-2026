~40 s. The grey bars are crowds and peak in summer. The blue line is risk per visit and peaks in winter. A June visit is about 5–6 times riskier than a January one, because seas are rougher and patrols mostly stop: about 80% of beach-months are patrolled in summer, about 12% in winter. This gap is what Task 2 targets.
