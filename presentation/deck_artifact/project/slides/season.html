~40 s. Top panel: crowds, which peak in January at about 4.8 million visits a year. Bottom panel: risk per visit, which peaks in June at 0.30 severe incidents per 100k visits, against 0.05 in January. So a June visit is about 5.5 times riskier, because seas are rougher and patrols mostly stop: about 80% of beach-months are patrolled from September to April, about 12% from May to August.
