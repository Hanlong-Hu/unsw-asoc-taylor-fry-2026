What has to hold for this plan to work

We assumed

  • Visitors grow 1.9% a year

  • Each initiative works as the Task 1 model says

Limitations

  • Association, not causation
  • Severe events are rare: wide ranges
  • The FY2020–21 dip is unexplained
  • 27 new drone beaches have never flown one
  • +68% drone hours needs operators and approvals
  • Lifeguard and flag effects are least certain

~30 s. Be honest and brief. Our forecast assumes steady visitor growth, normal weather and current safety levels. The main limits: we measure association, severe events are rare, and the FY2020–21 dip has no explanation in the data. On drones: about 5 of the 12.5 avoided come from 27 beaches that have never flown one, so we assume they respond like the 35 that have; and 20,000 extra flight hours is 68% more than today, which needs operators and approvals. Lifeguard and flag effects only show up between beaches, not within one; even if they did nothing the plan still avoids about 10 (A6). The 12-month review is built in to catch this.
