What has to hold for this plan to work

We assumed

  • Visitors grow 1.9% a year
  • Weather and seas are like the past decade
  • Existing safety stays at FY2026 levels
  • Each initiative works as the Task 1 model says

Limitations

  • Association, not causation
  • Severe events are rare, so ranges are wide
  • The FY2020–21 dip is unexplained
  • The first drone hours may be optimistic

The 12-month review is how we test these in practice.
