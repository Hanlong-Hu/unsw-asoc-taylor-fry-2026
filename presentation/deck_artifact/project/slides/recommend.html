This is the answer. $1.16M goes on drones: 27 new drones and about 20,000 extra flight hours. $392k funds new winter patrols at 22 beaches that have none from May to August. $330k runs summer education campaigns in 22 postcodes, and $115k puts rip flags on 23 beaches. Expected severe incidents fall from about 21 to about 9 a year: about 12.5 avoided, between 11 and 16. Drones do the most, about 8 of the 12.5. The top 10 beaches take about half the gain. Pause here.
