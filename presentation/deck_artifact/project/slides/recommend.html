This is the answer. $1.84M, 92% of the budget, buys 92 community first aid and rescue courses across 33 postcodes. $85k adds about 1,900 drone hours at 19 beaches that already fly drones, and $75k runs summer education campaigns in 5 postcodes. Together they cut expected severe incidents from about 19 to about 7 a year: about 13 avoided (11 to 15). First aid does most of the work, about 12 of the 13. Pause here.
