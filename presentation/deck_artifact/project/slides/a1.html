Model specification

One negative binomial GLM per severity: log E[incidents] = log(visitors) + month + weather + safety levers, with log(1 + hours) for lifeguards and drones, + visitor mix + beach traits (mild: one effect per beach instead).

Model choices

ChoiceWhat we used, and why
Count modelNegative binomial: variance over 10× the mean for mild; θ = 1.47 for severe
ExposureVisitors as an offset: a free slope forecast no better
Hourslog(1 + hours): diminishing returns, many zero months
Beach riskTraits for severe and close calls (best rolling score); one effect per beach for mild
ChallengerXGBoost: no clear gain, 12% too high on severe totals

A1

Q&A backup. Use if asked why a negative binomial model, how diminishing returns were modelled, or how beach effects were handled. A rate ratio is the factor on expected severe incidents for that change, holding the rest fixed: 0.30 for drones means about 70% fewer. Beach effects were tried for severe incidents but forecast badly out of sample (log score 0.34 vs 0.088), because 80 beach effects are too many for about 22 severe incidents a year.
