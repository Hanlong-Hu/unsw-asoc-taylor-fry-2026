A6

Q&A backup for "how sure are you?" Each bar is our plan scored under a different assumption. Within-beach: comparing each beach with itself. Lifeguards and flags do nothing: their effect only shows up between different beaches. Drones halved: in case the first drone hours are overstated. Pessimistic: drones halved, lifeguards at the within-beach value, no flag effect, all at once. Even then the plan avoids about 8 a year, and re-optimising for any of these cases would add at most 6%. Check on real outcomes: at the 35 beaches that started flying drones, the model expected 128 severe incidents without drones and 56 actually happened, about the cut the model predicts.
