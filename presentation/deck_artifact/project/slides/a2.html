Q&A backup. Use if asked how the model was tested, why XGBoost was not chosen, or how well calibrated the ranges are. Severe and mild ranges caught all five test years. Close calls are the weak spot: the model forecast too many in FY2023–25, so treat close-call numbers with care. XGBoost scores slightly better on the log score, most clearly for close calls, but for severe incidents it overpredicts totals by 12% and gives no effects Task 2 can use; the gain is within noise, so we kept the GLM.
